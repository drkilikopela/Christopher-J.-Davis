# Christopher J. Davis

**The Art of Decarbonization**

I work at the intersection of energy systems, sustainability, the fine arts, and community education. My interests center on a practical question: how can clean energy investments reduce operating costs while creating lasting value for the communities and institutions they serve?

I am pursuing an M.S. in Energy Systems Management at the University of San Francisco. My background includes program management and sustainability consulting, fleet electrification, and environmental education, with a growing research focus on museums and school districts.
